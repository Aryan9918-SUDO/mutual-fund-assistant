# Sample Q&A — Facts-Only MF Assistant

These are real responses from the assistant (offline/extractive mode; with the Gemini
key enabled the phrasing is smoother but the facts and citations are identical).
Every factual answer carries exactly one source link. Advice, performance and PII
queries are refused.

---

### 1. What is the expense ratio of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?
The Total Expense Ratio (Regular Plan) is 1.37% (inclusive of GST on management fees).
For the current plan-wise TER, refer to HDFC's Total Expense Ratio disclosure page.
**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/regular
_Last updated from sources: 2026-09-29_

### 2. What is the lock-in period for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver?
HDFC ELSS Tax Saver is an open-ended equity linked savings scheme (ELSS) with a
statutory lock-in of 3 years and a tax benefit. Investments qualify for deduction under
Section 80C up to Rs. 1.5 lakh.
**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver/regular
_Last updated from sources: 2026-09-29_

### 3. Minimum SIP for HDFC Mid Cap Fund?
Minimum SIP amount and minimum lump-sum/application amount are both Rs. 100.
**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-mid-cap-fund/regular
_Last updated from sources: 2026-09-29_

### 4. What is the exit load of HDFC Large Cap Fund?
Exit load is 1.00% if units are redeemed/switched out within 1 year from the date of
allotment, and nil after 1 year.
**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/product-solutions/overview/hdfc-top-100-fund/regular
_Last updated from sources: 2026-09-29_

### 5. What is the benchmark of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?
The benchmark of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is the NIFTY 500 (Total Returns Index). Its
riskometer level is 'Very High'.
**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/regular
_Last updated from sources: 2026-09-29_

### 6. What is a riskometer?
The Riskometer is a risk-measuring tool that depicts the risk level of a mutual fund
scheme across six levels — Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High and
Very High. SEBI mandates AMCs to display it for their schemes.
**Source:** https://investor.sebi.gov.in/riskometer.html
_Last updated from sources: 2026-09-29_

### 7. How do I download my capital gains statement?
A capital gains statement can be obtained via the AMC website or the registrars (RTAs)
CAMS and KFintech: log in with your PAN and registered email, request a consolidated
capital gains report, select the financial year, and receive it by email.
**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/learn/blog/how-get-capital-gain-statement-mutual-fund-schemes-india
_Last updated from sources: 2026-09-29_

### 8. How to download an account statement?
To download a Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from HDFC Mutual Fund: open the
Download/Consolidated Account Statement page, enter your PAN or folio number, request an
OTP, choose the financial year, and submit to receive it by email. There is no charge.
**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/consolidated-account-statement
_Last updated from sources: 2026-09-29_
